Family style and smorgasbord

Pennsylvania Dutch cooking is the farm food of Lancaster County's Amish, Mennonite and other German-speaking families: chicken pot pie with square noodles, chicken and waffles, ham balls, chow-chow, apple dumplings and shoofly pie. Many dining rooms serve it family style or as a smorgasbord, and many close on Sundays, which isn't closed.

The 6 on this list

They're in 2 regions of the state. 20 other places in the app's directory have “Dutch”, “Amish” or “smorgasbord” in their name; they're in the directory and the web search, but not on this list until we check them.

How the list was built: In October 2026 we looked up every place here in a 2025 or 2026 source (the shop's own site or menu, a dated post, local reporting, or a tourism listing) and matched its address. No current source, no listing. Review sites played no part, and the order is by region and city, not by anyone's rating. Menus and hours move, so call first.

Each place shows its official record where one is published in bulk, whether a Philadelphia place holds an active City food license, and elsewhere a link to the agency's own inspection search. In the app, this list puts the nearest places first, with Apple Maps' live card a tap away for hours and photos.

About inspections

In Philadelphia, “licensed” means an active City of Philadelphia L&I food license was matched to the place (OpenDataPhilly, City of Philadelphia License; licenses issued through Oct 11, 2026). It says the place is licensed, not how an inspection went. The Department of Public Health posts inspection reports one search at a time, with no grade or score: Philadelphia inspection reports. In the City's words:

“The City of Philadelphia makes no representation about the accuracy of any specific information in this data and is provided “as is” and without Warranty of any kind.”

Everywhere else, the Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture and about 100 local health departments post inspection results in one search, place by place: the PA Food Safety Inspection Search. We link to it and don't copy it, and there is no statewide grade. In the Department's words:

“Please remember that any inspection is a 'snapshot' of the day and time of the inspection. An inspection conducted on any given day may not be representative of the overall, long-term cleanliness of an establishment. Also, at the time of the inspection, violations are recorded but are often corrected on site prior to the inspector leaving the establishment.”
