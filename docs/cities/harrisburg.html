Harrisburg at a glance

Our directory counts 327 places to eat and drink in Harrisburg. Bar & Pub (46), Pizza (41), and Coffee & Café (33) lead the kinds of food. 101 are locations of a chain with five or more in Pennsylvania. None of our 105 hand-checked guide places is in Harrisburg or within 20 miles of it yet.

Health inspections here

In Dauphin County, the Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture or a local health department inspects, and the results are in the PA Food Safety Inspection Search, one search at a time; the app links to it. There is no statewide grade, and we don't make one.
