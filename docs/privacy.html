Privacy policy

Short version: the Pennsylvania Eats app collects nothing about you, and this website doesn't track you. Last updated Oct 26, 2026.

The app

  • No account, no analytics, no ads. The app has no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting code. We receive no data from it.
  • Location. If you allow it, your location is used on your device to sort places by distance and show where you are on the map. It is never sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • Saved places and filters are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Apple Maps. When you open a place's hours and photos, or ask for directions, the app asks Apple Maps for that place. Apple handles that request under Apple's privacy policy, as it does for any app that shows a map.
  • Data updates. When you open the app, it checks this website for newer restaurant data, at most once a day (or an hour later if a check didn't go through), and downloads it when there is some. These requests carry no location or identifiers. GitHub, which hosts this site, may keep standard server logs of them, as it does for any visit here.
  • Spotlight. The app may add its hand-checked places to your device's search index so you can find them from Spotlight. That index stays on your device.
  • Calls, websites, and email you start from a place open in the Phone app, your browser or Mail, and are handled by them.

On the App Store, the app's privacy label will be "Data Not Collected".

This website

The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, fonts, maps, or scripts from anyone else. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The web search keeps three things in your browser's local storage, on your device only: the places you save, the region each saved place is in (so the right file loads), and the last guide you opened. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them. If you tap "Use my location", open the "Near me" guide, or sort by "Nearest", your browser asks first, and your location is used in the page to sort by distance. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to fix the listing you told us about. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your address.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: nick@eatsranked.com.
